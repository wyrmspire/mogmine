## Level-set fixture

The `mogmine.levelset.v1` assistant draft for Yahoo `MES=F` is revision 1, computed at 08:05 CT. It records provider and symbol, the session date, calculation version, provenance, the reference quote, calculation inputs and zone tolerance. Its 25 `levels` can be point prices or ranges (`price_low`/`price_high`), with stable keys, labels, formulas and optional formation times. Its 13 `zones` group those keys and `plan` references the zones. The fixture has mixed computed and authored levels, including a weekly high zone. It contains a prior daily settlement of 7640.50 and a distinct 15:00 CT close of 7642.75. The draft is currently available as a fixture. [MES-2026-11-12.json](../fixtures/levelsets/MES-2026-11-12.json)

## Core math in this slice

- Use native MES/MNQ bars, the regular New York session schedule and an explicit UTC `asOf`. Only completed candles contribute. Return interval coverage and withhold values for a gapped or misaligned window. Holiday gaps remain visible through coverage until the exchange calendar is decided.
- Apply the fixture's prior Globex definition for PDH/PDL, compute overnight extremes through 09:30 ET or the cutoff, and compute current-week extremes beginning with Sunday's open. Read PDC only from an explicitly supplied, correctly dated prior-session settlement candle whose close was available by the cutoff. A daily close used as a settlement proxy needs that provenance and a normalized session date before it reaches core math. [Session and settlement definitions](12-levels-zones-and-daily-markup.md#2-sessions) · [CME settlement procedure](https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/ser/2020/09/SER-8591.pdf)
- Require the caller to pick the VWAP anchor while `OD-18` is open. Use cumulative volume-weighted HLC3. Zero-volume candles hold the last VWAP once it exists. The session profile uses the same window and allocates each bar's volume to its nearest HLC3 tick. Take the highest-volume tick as POC, then expand over neighboring occupied ticks until the area contains at least 70% of volume. Resolve volume ties toward the lower price. The profile is an OHLCV estimate under `OD-53`. [Volume profile terminology](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000502040-volume-profile-indicators-basic-concepts/)
- Return signed price-to-VWAP distance in points and percent, VWAP slope in points per minute over a caller-selected number of intervals, and distance to every point or range with strictly nearest above and below. A hit on a level or inside a zone has zero distance.
- Keep the result a versioned calculation snapshot. It includes the extreme's bar-open time and the time the calculation became available. Carry provider, contract and data revision with the inputs when an app consumer creates a reproducible view. Fixture revisions, authored levels, merged zones and the daily plan belong to the separate level-set document.
