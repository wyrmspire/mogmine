## Choices I made (redirect if wrong)

1. **Slice position.** The package prompt says "append" the batch-2 candidates. Appending them at the end of the 13-item list would never fill the 12-slice budget: with 0–4 trigger consumers, the first 12 unused candidates cover everything before the tail. So I placed the four batch-2 entries after the range group (`contextRanges`) and before `openingRange`, `sessionClock` and `vwapRelation`. Volume slices stay first, and the lower-priority clock and VWAP slices are the ones that drop out.
2. **Batch-2 candidates use default params.** `rangeRegime`, `emaRelation` (minutes 1) and `profileRelation` (anchor rth) and `gapOpen` are referenced without explicit params, so they match the skill defaults.
3. **Test fixture.** The integration tests build 22 synthetic weekday sessions (1,380 bars each, seeded random walk). That clears the 5-session range warm-up and the 700-bar EMA warm-up within the run. The lookahead test samples four decision bars across the last sessions.
