## Choices I made (redirect if wrong)

1. **EMA track.** One memoized pass per `(series, minutes)` in a `WeakMap`. Index `k` is the k-th completed timeframe bar. For 1m, `completed[i] = i + 1`. For 5m, ET-aligned buckets count as complete when their end is `<= time[i] + barMs`, and the bucket close is its last present 1m bar. Values at bar `i` depend only on bars `<= i`, which the lookahead harness confirms.
2. **Warm-up.** `ema200` and `ema20` are exposed only once 700 completed bars exist (the `computeEma200` rule). The 20 EMA is seeded from the first 20 closes.
3. **`slope200` is `null`** (with a note) when the earlier point `k - slopeBars` is not itself past warm-up. The spec did not say what to do here; I chose null over a warm-up skip of the whole result.
4. **Side bands.** `"at"` is `|ticks| <= 2` inclusive. `"above-far"` / `"below-far"` are strictly beyond `farTicks`.
5. **Profile.** Mirrors `volumeProfile` in `session-indicators.ts` (same tick binning, same first-peak POC, same expansion tie rule). Bars with `NaN` or `0` volume are skipped. `volume-missing` fires when `missing * 5 > length` (exact integer form of `> 20%`) or total valid volume is 0. The RTH gate is `time[i] < rthOpen` → `not-known-yet`.
6. **Profile window scan** is O(window) per bar and not memoized (about 390 bars RTH, 1380 globex). Recipe scans will be slower than the EMA path. Say if that needs a memo too.
