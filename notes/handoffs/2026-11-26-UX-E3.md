# UX-E3 — Partial-lot envelope zone with 1 px lot exit separators

Spec: `docs/design-2026-11-24/08-visual-quality.md` §2 F-4, §5 (Partial lots), §7.3 UX-E3.

## What changed

- `lib/lot-envelope.ts` (new, 134 lines, pure, relative `.ts` imports only). `buildActualLotEnvelopes(zones)` groups same-tone `actual` lots that overlap or touch (`a.right >= b.left - 1`).
  - Single lot: one envelope, exact rect, `entryY = y0`, `farY = y1`, one far-edge segment at `y1` at `edgeActual` (0.90), `separators: []`. A lone partial lot keeps its own `opacityMultiplier`, so it renders as before.
  - Multi-lot: `slices` are non-overlapping column slices across the union of x intervals (zero overlapping fills). `entryY` is the earliest lot's entry, `farY` the exit furthest from it. One gradient, full strength, no per-lot `opacityMultiplier`. Far-edge rules at `farY` (0.90). One 1 px separator per lot whose exit is not `farY` (alpha 0.65, solid), spanning that lot's x range.
- `components/PositionBoxPrimitive.ts`: actual zones are projected once and grouped into envelopes. The plan fills are cut with `subtractRects` against the envelope slices. Actual lots are no longer painted per mark. Envelope fill and edges are painted in one pass each. Zone-label placement moved into a shared helper, used by plan zones and envelopes. The plan-only edge and fill branches are kept. `archived`, `hatch`, `segment`, `flag`, `bracket`, `priceAxisViews()`, `setExportMode()` and `hitTestArchivedBoxes` are unchanged.
- `lib/__tests__/lot-envelope.test.ts` (new, 107 lines): 5 tests.
- Not touched: `lib/trade-marks.ts`, `components/TradeDetail.tsx`, PR #71, PR #265.
