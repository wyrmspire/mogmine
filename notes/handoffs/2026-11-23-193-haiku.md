## Open items for the reviewer

1. **Sample-trade children reach the server (real gap, not fixed).** The migration has no foreign key from
   `mogmine_mood_records.trade_id`, `mogmine_calls.trade_id` or `mogmine_prompt_dismissals.trade_id` to `mogmine_trades`. The only
   cascade is a trigger on a trade delete on the server. Samples never reach the server, so a mood score, call or dismissal recorded
   against a sample trade would be pushed as an orphan row. The brief's "samples never sync" rule covers trades only.
   The fix needs the set of sample trade ids, which these stores do not hold. Suggested shape: a `sampleTradeIds` source passed in
   from `lib/store.ts`'s wiring, then `include` excludes children of samples. Not verified in the UI.
2. **Mood spoke registry stays device-only**, as the brief asked. It needs singleton sync, which another slice builds.
3. **Suggestions** are never synced (the contract's `derivedNeverStored`).
4. **Not verified in a browser.** No 390×844 check and no real Supabase call (no network, by brief).
5. **Tests are not mutation-checked.** Each test passes against the wired code; I did not remove a `changed()` call to confirm
   the test then fails.
