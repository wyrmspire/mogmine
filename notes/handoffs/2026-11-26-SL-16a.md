## Choices the spec did not settle

1. **Prior-session walk.** Prior sessions come from `series.sessions` (the sessions that have bars or drops), walked backward from today. A session counts only if it is not `excluded`, has `expectedBars > 0`, and has `presentBars >= 50%` of `expectedBars`. A day with no bars at all is not in the list, so it is skipped silently rather than counted as a gap.
2. **Session close.** The "closing price" of a session is the close of its last present bar. `SessionInfo.open` and `close` are timestamps, not prices.
3. **`trendRegime` series.** The close sequence is the prior closes plus `close[i]`, which is in progress. It has `n` steps. `multiDayAvgRange` is the mean prior-session range, excluding today. The multi-session `[low, high]` includes today's bars up to `i`. The slope is an OLS fit of closes against position (0 = oldest prior).
4. **Share test.** "At least 60%" is applied as `5 × count >= 3 × steps`, which avoids float error. Flat steps count toward neither side.
5. **`rangeRegime` validation.** `minSessions > sessions` returns `bad-params`. The spec did not say this. Without it, the skill would always return warmup.
6. **`adrExtension` pre-open rule.** The spec says `decisionMinute < 570`. I used `bar close < that session's 09:30 ET`. The two agree on normal session-day bars. They differ on Globex-evening bars (18:00–23:59 ET) that belong to the next session. Under the spec's literal rule, those bars would reach the anchor check and return `coverage`. My rule returns `not-known-yet`, which is correct because the 09:30 open has not happened yet.
7. **`gapOpen` 09:31 rule.** Same reasoning: the check compares the decision time with the session's 09:31 ET, not with `decisionMinute`. The rule is also correct on evening bars.
8. **`gapOpen` filled scan.** Scans RTH bars from `i` back to the 09:30 timestamp. Uses the level book's `rth_open` when present, otherwise the 09:30 bar's open.
9. **`adrExtension` windows.** `windowHigh` and `windowLow` run over the anchor through `i`. For `session` that is the whole session to date. For `rth` it is 09:30 to `i`. The anchor is the session's first present bar open, or the 09:30 bar open.
10. **Output rounding.** Points and ratios are rounded to 6 decimals with `roundTo`. Classification uses the unrounded value.
11. **Meta `lookbackMinutes`.** `rangeRegime` and `adrExtension` declare 20×1440, `trendRegime` 15×1440, `gapOpen` 390. These are documentation values only.
