Existing trades keep their saved R."* Respects Shield mode by hiding `$` chips/values and preserving `account.r_size_cents` on save.

4. **Daily Session Loop polish (`components/TodayScreen.tsx`, `components/ReviewScreen.tsx`, `components/DayReviewScreen.tsx`, `app/review-day.css`)**
