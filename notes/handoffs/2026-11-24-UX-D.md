## Decisions

- The predicate uses `== null`, so `undefined` also counts as "no setup". The prompt's `=== null` would miss legacy records.
- The hint sits after the `Deeper review` details, so it is under all four notes fields. It also shows in the compact queue card.
- The queue card "No setup" chip is hidden while sealed (blind review), as the spec asks.
