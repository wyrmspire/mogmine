## What it does

- Candidates: closed MES trades whose `playbook_id` equals `recipe.playbookId`. Samples are excluded unless `includeSamples` is true (default false); the excluded count is reported. A recipe with no playbook tags nothing.
- Decision bar: the bar that closed at or before the entry time (§4.4). An entry at 09:45:00 ET is decided by the 09:44 bar.
- Per row: `window`, `side`, and each trigger leaf (flattened with the evaluator's own paths, `trigger.all[0]`, …) each with `passed` and the `actual` value. `fits` = all of them pass.
- Real result R = `tradePnl(trade)` dollars ÷ the fixed R size passed in (`rSize.usd`). Never a stop width. Unpriceable trades count in `n` but not in averages.
- Fit vs not-fit: n, priced count, avg R, total R.
- Older than the loaded 1m history: counted and listed by id, not computed. After the last bar: counted. In range with no usable 1m decision bar: `noBarAtEntry`.
- Headline: "7 tagged trades in range · 3 meet the recipe · real avg +0.40R on those 3 · −0.22R on the other 4 · N older than the bar history".

## Choices made (reversible)

1. **Fit includes window and side**, not only trigger conditions. A long idea taken at 14:00 or a short on a long recipe is not "the recipe".
2. **Simulated outcome comes from the run's `matches`**, matched by decision-bar index. `buildTrade` in `runner.ts` is private, so an off-trigger simulation is not possible without exporting it from SL-8's file. A fitting trade that the run's dedupe skipped shows no simulated line.
3. **Per-leaf pass/fail** uses `evaluateCondition` on each leaf, so `crossesAbove` and similar keep their previous-bar semantics. The fit verdict is the same as the runner's.
