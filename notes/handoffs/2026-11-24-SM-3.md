## Decisions and limits

- `riskR` and `resultR` are untouched. The test checks that `riskR` stays on the initial stop after a widen.
- `widestRisk` and `lockedR` use the trade's own tick value and quantity, and divide by the R size passed in (`D-A`).
- `lockedR` does not require a closed trade. It reports the stop as it stands at exit (`stop_moves` last, else `stop_loss_price`).
- The slippage note copy says "past" whatever the side of the exit, as 05 §3.2 specifies.
- **Not done:** the 02 §2.6 `initial_stop_time_defaulted` warning is not returned. `parseTrade` has no warnings channel, and the API response shape belongs to API-2b/API-3. The default itself is in place.
- `StopFacts` reuses the existing `.initial-stop` class (`app/globals.css` is read-only here).
- **Watch for the planner:** the new last-move rule applies to every command-layer write. A stored trade whose `stop_loss_price` differs from its last stop move will now refuse `update`. The UI's own store path may not go through this layer. Not checked here.
