# SM-2 — Untimed stop moves without false SL touches

No migration. Nothing applied to Supabase.

## What changed

- **`SM-UT-1/2` (`lib/trade-document.ts`):** `EventBasis` gains `"untimed"`; `isBasis` accepts it. `pickStopMoveTime` deleted. An unrecorded move (`initial_stop_price ≠ stop_loss_price`, no `stop_moves`):
  - closed trade → `modify` at `exit_time`, basis `untimed` (never `initial_stop_set_at`);
  - open trade → no modify. The initial stop stays the working stop across the hold (see decisions);
  - stop removed (`stop_loss_price` null) → `cancel`, basis `untimed`, at `exit_time ?? entry_time`.
- **`SM-UT-2` (`lib/trade-bar-validation.ts`):** stop-touch loop skips `stop.basis === "untimed"`. The initial stop is checked across the hold.
- **Chart (`lib/trade-marks.ts`):** `combineBasis` treats `untimed` as `inferred`. The existing stepped-path code draws the dashed step at the exit bar. Its label is `moved · time?` at medium/high, omitted at low. `PositionBoxPrimitive.ts` is unchanged: `untimed` already gets the segment's `dash` (dashed) and needs no special case.
- **`SM-UT-3` (`lib/data-notes.ts`):** optional `untimedStopMovePrice` on `DataNoteInput`. Emits a quiet note with the exact SM-UT-3 text and `action: { kind: "edit_untimed_stop", price }`. `DataNoteAction` is now a union.
- **Addendum (planned boxes):** no change needed in `lib/trade-marks.ts` or `components/TradeChart.tsx` (see below).

## Decisions I made (planner can redirect)

1. **Open trades with an unrecorded move:** no modify event. The initial stop stays the working stop across the hold. The brief's "untimed modify at latest timestamp" conflicts with "checked across the hold": a modify at entry would stop the initial stop's segment at entry. Consequence: for an open trade, `stop_path` shows the initial stop; `stop_loss_price` is still on the record but not in the derived path.
2. **Removed stop (`stop_loss_price` null):** `cancel` basis `untimed` at `exit_time ?? entry_time`. The entry fallback is the existing behaviour for this edge case, kept so the removed-stop fact is not lost.
