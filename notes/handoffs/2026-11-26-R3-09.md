# R3-09: live accounts and prefs into the R selectors on the screens

Wave 3, agent r3-3. Branch `r3/R3-09`. Spec: MOGMINE-BLUEPRINT §2.6, docs/design-2026-11-24/04-r-size §3.2/§3.5.

## What changed

Each screen now reads `useAccounts()` and `usePrefs()` and passes `{ accounts: accounts.accounts, prefs }` into the R-consuming selector. Accounts and prefs are in the memo deps where the call is memoized.

- `components/TodayScreen.tsx`: `groupByDay(trades, { accounts, prefs })`.
- `components/FeedScreen.tsx`: `groupByDay(shown, { accounts, prefs })`. `useAccounts` was already imported.
- `components/RunningStatsCard.tsx`: `runningStats(trades, now, { accounts, prefs })`.
- `components/DrillScreen.tsx`: a memoised `rSize` feeds `missesDeck` and `takeVsPass`. `DrillCard` passes `{ accounts, prefs }` to `isMiss`.
- `components/PlaybookBinder.tsx`: `binderView(playbooks, trades, records, now, { accounts, prefs })`.
- `components/MoodCard.tsx`: a memoised `rSize` goes into `similarTrades` and `moodOutcomeStats` as `rSize`.
- `lib/__tests__/ui-r-convergence.test.ts` (new): source-wiring checks for all six files, plus a selector check that a $300 account trade with `r_size_cents: null` and +$75 gives +0.25R in `groupByDay`, `runningStats`, `binderView`, `takeVsPass` and `moodOutcomeStats`.
