## 1. Summary of Changes

Implemented pointer hit-testing and tap interaction for archived neighbouring trades on `TradeChart`:

1. **`components/PositionBoxPrimitive.ts`**:
   - Exported pure helper `hitTestArchivedBoxes(x, y, rects, tolerance?)` (and alias `hitTestArchivedMark`) that returns the matching `tradeId` or `null`.
   - Supports boundary touches (inclusive edges), negative/inverted dimension normalization, touch tolerance expansion, and topmost (reverse-order / last-rendered) precedence for overlapping rects.
   - Tracked rendered screen coordinates of `layer === "archived"` marks in `PositionBoxPrimitive.archivedRects` during canvas paint passes (supporting both zone boxes and scratch segments).
   - Implemented `ISeriesPrimitive.hitTest(x, y): PrimitiveHoveredItem | null` (with `externalId: tradeId`, `cursorStyle: "pointer"`) and direct convenience helper `hitTestTradeId(x, y): string | null`.
   - Added `resolveNeighbourTradeId(mark)` to link archived marks with their `tradeId` via explicit mark attributes or matched `NeighbourTradeProjection[]`.

2. **`components/TradeChart.tsx`**:
   - Added optional `onSelectNeighbour?: (tradeId: string) => void` and `neighbourTrades?: readonly NeighbourTradeProjection[]` props.
   - Wired `chart.subscribeClick` to test click/tap points against archived boxes and invoke `onSelectNeighbour(tradeId)` when hit.
   - Cleaned up click subscription in unmount cleanup (`chart.unsubscribeClick`).
   - Kept archived boxes faint (`<= 0.1` alpha, dotted outline, zero text labels or axis tags per `D-010`) while pan, crosshair scrub, and pinch-zoom remain completely untouched.

3. **`lib/__tests__/neighbour-hit-test.test.ts`**:
   - 11 unit tests covering interior hits, boundary hits, 1px misses on all sides, overlapping rect resolution, inverted dimensions, touch tolerance, empty/null safety, non-finite coordinates, and `PositionBoxPrimitive` lifecycle.
