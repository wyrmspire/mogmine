# SL-6 — Strategy Lab measures (M1–M3) and R size

Branch: `sl/SL-6` (from `origin/main`). Spec: `docs/design-2026-11-24/09-strategy-lab.md` §4.1, §4.4, §6.4, §9 (M1–M3), §14 row SL-6.

## Done

- **M1 `bracketOutcome` v1.0.0** (`measureBracket` plus the skill wrapper). Simulates a stop, a target, and a max-hold or flat exit from the trigger close. It walks only the bars after the trigger, so the trigger bar's own range is never inside the hold (R-POST-1). The outcome carries `exitReason`, `exitTime`, `pnlPoints`, `pnlUsd`, `resultR`, `riskMultiple`, `mfePoints`, `maePoints`, `mfeR`, `maeR`, `minutesToExit`, and `minutesToTarget`. `riskMultiple` (P&L ÷ stop distance) has its own label and is never called R.
- **M2 `levelReach` v1.0.0.** Whether price reached a level on the target side before the stop, within the hold. A stop and a touch in the same bar count as the stop.
- **M3 `forwardReturn` v1.0.0.** Signed points from the trigger close to the close N minutes later. It uses the nearest earlier present bar within 2 minutes, else `null`.
- **R size (`lib/strategy-lab/r.ts`).** `labRSize` delegates to the merged `lib/r-size.ts` `resolveRSize`: the account's R size, then `prefs.riskBudgetUsd`, then $250. `rBasisLabel` gives the once-per-screen basis line, for example `R = $300 (SIM Eval 50K)`.
- **Tests:** `lib/__tests__/strategy-lab-measure.test.ts`, 21 tests. Expected values are hand-computed in the test comments.

## Choices the planner should record in DECISIONS.md (open items)

1. **MFE/MAE sign.** Favourable-positive, measured from the fill `close[i]`. MFE ≥ 0 and MAE ≤ 0. Both are computed over the bars after the trigger, up to and including the exit bar.
2. **Deviation from R-POST-2.** `docs/05` says MAE and MFE are null when fewer than two inside bars remain. The `Outcome` type has no nullable MFE or MAE, so this package computes them from the fill price and the bars that were walked. The rule is not applied here. Raise it if the trade-level null rule should also apply to lab outcomes.
3. **Flat time is an integer.** `flatMinuteEt` is minutes after midnight ET (default 1019 = 16:59), not an "HH:MM" string, because `ParamSpec` has no time type. The runner converts the recipe's `flatAtEt`.
4. **Exit reasons.** The flat exit, and a session that runs out before the hold, are both `session-end`. The max-hold exit is `time`. Within a bar, stop and target are checked before any time or flat exit.
5. **Exit time** is the close of the exit bar. Intra-bar order is unknown at 1-minute resolution.
6. **Gaps.** A gap longer than 15 minutes inside the walk is `coverage`. Shorter gaps are skipped.
7. **Entry price** is `close[i]` rounded to the tick. Stop and target must already sit on the tick grid, or the call returns `bad-params`.
8. **Registration.** The three measures are exported as `MEASURE_SKILLS`. They are not added to `SKILLS` in `registry.ts`, because that file is not in SL-6's file list. SL-8 (the runner) registers them. `conditions.ts` already rejects measures in a trigger; a test covers this.
9. **Money** is in integer ticks and cents: `tickValueCents` = `pointValueUsd × tickSize × 100` (125 for MES). Only the final ratios are floats.

## Verification

Lookahead harness for after-entry measures. `assertNoLookahead` from the SL-1 harness rejects measures by design, so this package tests the after-entry rule directly:

- The outcome is identical when the series is truncated at the exit bar. A poison bar after the exit has no effect.
- The walk does need the bar that closes the hold: truncating one bar earlier returns `coverage`.
- Mutating the trigger bar's own range, and any bar before the trigger, leaves the outcome unchanged.
- The same check holds for `levelReach`, which is tested with truncation at its touch bar.
