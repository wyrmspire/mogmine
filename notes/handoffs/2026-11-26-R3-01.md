## What changed

- **Split.** Pure target and lot-band geometry moved to `lib/partials-geometry.ts`: `PartialExit`, `PartialEntryInput`, `PartialTarget`, `PartialLotBand`, `CalculateTargetsParams`, direction/tick helpers, `calculateRiskTicks`, `calculatePlannedTargets`, `calculateWeightedPlannedRewardRisk` (renamed from `calculateWeightedPlannedR`), `isScratchPartials`, and the FIFO band builders (`closedLotBand`, `openLotBand`). `lib/partials.ts` keeps the summary types, FIFO matching, and `calculatePartials`, and re-exports everything public so there is one entry point. Relative `./…ts` imports only.
- **Account R (D-A).** `rSizeCents?` on `CalculatePartialsParams` and `CalculateTargetsParams`. Omitted or invalid values fall back to `resolveRSize({ account_id: null }).cents`, which is the repo default ($250). Callers can pass `30_000` for a $300 R.
- **Renames.** `PartialTarget.plannedR` -> `plannedRewardRisk` and new `plannedAccountR`. `PartialLotBand.r` -> `riskMultiple`, `rContribution` -> `riskMultipleContribution`, new `accountR`. `PartialSummary` drops `rMultiple`, `realizedR`, `closedR` and adds `rSizeCents`, `resultR`, `realizedAccountR`, `riskR`, `riskMultiple`, `closedRiskMultiple`.
- **No `R` on stop-width ratios.** `.rMultiple` and `.plannedR` occur zero times in `lib/partials.ts` and `lib/partials-geometry.ts`.
- **Scratch (D-C).** `isScratchPartials` logic is unchanged: max(0.1 × the trade's own stop distance, 2 ticks).
- **Integer math.** Ticks and cents throughout. Point values come from `specFor` and `tickValueCents`; no `$5` literal.
