# SL-5 — Volume skills V1–V5

Branch: `sl/SL-5` (from `origin/main`)
Spec: `docs/design-2026-11-24/09-strategy-lab.md` §7.3–7.5, §9 (V1–V5), §14 row SL-5

## Done

- **V1 `rvol`** (feature, v1.0.0): today's window ÷ the median of the same ET-clock window over the previous `sessions` sessions. Buckets low / normal / high / very-high at 0.7 / 1.3 / 2. Prior-session lookup is by ET minute, not UTC, so it stays correct across DST.
- **V2 `volumeSpike`** (label, v1.0.0): ratio of volume at `i` to the median of the previous valid bars, a log z-score, and `spike = ratio ≥ multiple`. Missing prior bars are skipped, and the window may cross the 17:00 halt.
- **V3 `volumeAtLevel`** (feature, v1.0.0, degraded, warning on whole-bar attribution): volume of bars that trade within `toleranceTicks` of a level since the session or RTH open, plus separate visits and a share bucket.
- **V4 `entryVolume`** (feature, v1.0.0): entry-bar volume, the k bars before it, the least-squares trend (flat within 5% of the mean per bar), and entry ÷ median.
- **V5 `deltaProxy`** (feature, v1.0.0, degraded, `proxy: true`): CLV or tick-direction proxy delta, normalised to −1..1 with a ±0.1 flat band. Carries the required "Not real order flow" warning.
- All five are exported as `VOLUME_SKILLS`. Each has preflight and compute, and both reject bad parameters as `bad-params`.
- Missing volume (NaN, from volume 0) is never counted as activity. Every skill reads only bars at or before `i`. The lookahead harness runs on all five: V1, V2 and V4 at three decision bars in a shared test with future bars present, and V3 and V5 at one bar each in their own tests.

## Decisions taken (the spec left these open; reversible)

1. **Category labels:** rvol `low|normal|high|very-high`; volumeSpike none; volumeAtLevel share `low|mid|high`; entryVolume trend `rising|falling|flat` and bucket `low|normal|high`; deltaProxy sign `positive|flat|negative`.
2. **V3 share buckets are fixed thirds** (1/3, 2/3), not terciles of history. History terciles would need a cross-session pass and are not needed for the MVP.
3. **V2 log z-score** uses the population standard deviation. A flat window is detected by exact equality, and `logZ` is then `null` with a note. The floating-point sd is about 1e-15, so a threshold test would be unreliable.
4. **V1 baseline** takes the previous `sessions` sessions and skips unusable ones. It does not extend further back to refill the count.
5. **V3 missing-volume bars** are excluded entirely. They neither add volume nor break or extend a visit, and the count is noted.
6. **V4 `k` prior bars** are looked up by time, so a missing minute inside them is `volume-missing`. The `medianBars` median uses valid bars, and needs at least `min(20, medianBars)` of them, otherwise `warmup`.
7. **V5 tick method**: a bar whose previous minute is absent contributes zero, and the carried sign does not change.
8. **V3 `level` parameter** defaults to `""`. It is required, so an empty or unknown key is `bad-params`, never a silent default such as PDH.

## Known limits (for the reviewer)

- **18:00 ET open:** a window that reaches back across the 17:00–18:00 halt has no bars before 18:00, so V1, V4 and V5 return `volume-missing` for the first few minutes of each session. This is honest but noisy. The §9 wording ("missing bar → volume-missing") was followed literally rather than treating the halt as structural.
- The mark's `knownAt` is `bar-close` for all five. None of them is a measure, so the `after-entry` path does not apply.
