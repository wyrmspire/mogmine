## Rules encoded

- R is always the run's fixed account R size. The basis line comes from `rSizeBasis` (`R = $300 (SIM Eval 50K)`, or `R = $250 (default risk budget)`). Shield keeps R and drops the dollar sign. Results are never expressed as 1R.
- Buckets: n ≥ 10 full; n 5–9 greyed and marked "thin" (no rank); n < 5 "counts only" with dashes for every rate.
- Thin separators stay visible but greyed.
- Missing and NaN values print "n/a". Volume 0 is mapped to NaN upstream, so it never prints as 0.
- MFE and MAE are shown as multiples of risk, and only as the engine supplies them.
- The multiple-comparison line is always rendered. It is also rendered when there are zero slices.
