## Choices I made (redirect if wrong)

1. **Warm-up sessions for 15m = 8, not 2–3.** 700 × 15 min ≈ 10,500 min, about 7.6 Globex sessions of 23 h. 5m needs 3. 1m needs 1 (prior session alone is 1,380 bars). 1h needs about 31, so it skips loading and reports the short history. Cap is 10 sessions.
2. **1m history is filtered to 1m base bars.** Mixing 5m bars into a 1m series would draw them as 1m. Coarser charts aggregate any base.
3. **`computeEma200` has no `symbol` parameter.** The session calendar is the equity-index (MES/MNQ) one; nothing else needs it.
4. **`ChartTheme.ema200` is optional.** `lib/shareCard.ts` builds `DEFAULT_CARD_THEME: ChartTheme` as a literal, and that file is not writable here. `TradeChart` falls back to `#38bdf8`. Same pattern as `levelLabelBgAlpha`.
5. **Token name.** The spec's `--ind-ema200` is used. The repo's existing VWAP token is `--indicator-vwap`, so the two names differ.
6. **Point times are bar open times (UTC ms)** everywhere, matching `Candle` and `VwapPoint`. The API's cut is by bar close (`time + 1m <= as_of`), applied through the bars it already filters.
7. **1m older than 30 days** is detected from the entry time and short-circuits the fetch with `1m bars older than 30 days are unavailable from Yahoo`.
