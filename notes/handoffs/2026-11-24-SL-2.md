# SL-2 — Strategy Lab bar loader, IDB cache, session coverage

Branch: `sl/SL-2`

## Done

- Added `lib/strategy-lab/bars.ts`: one-session-at-a-time loader for `/api/market-data` (symbol `MES`).
  - Session window = `equityIndexSessionBounds(date)`: 18:00 ET the previous day to 17:00 ET. DST-correct, never a fixed offset. Covered by a 23 h test on winter, summer, and both DST-change nights.
  - Response cleaning: rows with non-numeric prices or times are dropped; rows outside `[from, to)` are dropped. Volume `0` or missing is kept as `null` or `0`; `buildBarSeries` turns it into `NaN` (missing, not activity).
  - Excluded sessions with a typed reason, never a throw: `not-1m` (a 5m or 1h answer, detail "only 5m left at Yahoo"), `no-data` (404, or an empty 200 such as a weekend), `upstream` (5xx or network error), `bad-response` (not JSON, no candles array, or non-empty candles without a trustworthy timeframe).
  - IDB cache under `strategy-lab:v1:MES:<date>`. Only settled 1m sessions are written (close more than 1 h in the past). Today is never read or written. A corrupt cache entry is a miss.
  - Concurrency pool, default 3, results in input order, `onProgress(done, total)` after each session.
  - `loadBarSeries(dates, deps)` returns the built `BarSeries` (loaded sessions only), per-session load results, and per-session volume integrity.
  - `volumeIntegrity(series, date)` grades the 390 RTH minutes (09:30–16:00 ET): `ok` at zero missing; `degraded` at or below 2%; `missing` above 2%. A missing bar counts as volume-missing; `absent` is the subset with no bar at all.
  - `recentSessionDates(endDate, count)` returns the last N regular sessions, oldest first, skipping weekends.
- Added `lib/__tests__/strategy-lab-bars.test.ts`: 14 tests with mocked fetch and cache. No network, no real IndexedDB.

## Choices made (inside scope, reversible)

- The loader is in `bars.ts`. It defines its own `SessionLoad`, `VolumeIntegrity`, and `LabBarData` types, so `types.ts` stays frozen.
- The session `to` bound is exclusive, matching Yahoo's `period2`.
- Missing-volume grading counts absent RTH minutes as volume-missing, because they have no volume either.
- Only 1m sessions are cached. A 5m result is never stored, so a later retry can still get 1m.
- The lookahead harness is not used here: SL-2 has no skills. The harness applies from SL-3.
