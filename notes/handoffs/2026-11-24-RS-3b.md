## Decisions made in the package (flagging for review)

- **Ruler labels.** Lines stay at whole multiples `k` of the stop width, so spacing is unchanged. Labels are `formatAccountR(k × accountRiskR)`. `accountRiskR == null` falls back to 1, per spec. `formatAccountR` gives `+0.25R`, `+0.5R`, `+1.0R`, and `−1.0R` (U+2212, matching `formatR`).
- **Target label.** When `accountRiskR` is set, the label is account R (`TP +0.75R`). When it is null, the old stop-width label is kept (`TP 2.0R`). This keeps existing `trade-marks.test.ts` goldens intact. It is a known inconsistency: see the out-of-scope note below.
- **Caption.** `rStripCaption(trade, layout, accountRSizeCents?)` computes exit and plan as `tradePnl(price) × 100 ÷ cents`. Labels are account R. A 0.25R trade with a 2:1 target and exit at target gives `Ran to plan, exit +0.5R, plan +0.5R`.
- **`StripTrade`** gains optional `symbol`, `quantity`, `account_id`, `r_size_cents`. `RStrip.tsx` (read-only) passes this type, and its typecheck needs it.
- **AddTradeSheet result R.** `resultR` requires a full `TradeRecord` (it checks `exit_time`), and the draft is not one. The sheet computes `pnl × 100 ÷ rSize.cents` inline, with the same definition. Widening `resultR`'s parameter in `lib/r-size.ts` would remove the duplication. That file is out of scope.
- **r-trailed-stop caption golden** changed from `exit 1.2R, plan 2.7R` to `exit +1.8R, plan +4.2R`. Verified by hand: 2 lots × 45 pts × $5 = $450, and $450 ÷ $250 = 1.8R. Plan: 2 × 105 × $5 = $1050, ÷ $250 = 4.2R.

## Out-of-scope notes (not edited)

- `components/ReviewChart.tsx` and `components/PlaybookCard.tsx` call `buildMarks` without `accountRiskR`. Their ruler and target labels still show stop-width values. They should pass `accountRiskR: riskR(trade, resolveRSize(...))`.
- `components/RStrip.tsx` calls `rStripCaption(trade, layout)` without an R size. It therefore resolves the trade's frozen `r_size_cents`, else the default ($250). A trade on an account with a different R size and no frozen size gets the wrong caption denominator. Passing an account-resolved size requires editing `RStrip.tsx`.
