## 5. Known gaps and decisions for the planner

- **Placeholder rule is split.** `hasRealVolume` treats all-`1` as not real (the spec's rule). The VWAP gate in `session-indicators.ts` keeps its finite and >= 0 check, so a synthetic all-`1` window would still be weighted. Nothing in the app produces that now, and changing the gate would alter existing mixed-volume goldens (`session-indicators.test.ts`), which is out of this package's files.
- **1 px bar gap:** not set explicitly. lightweight-charts sizes histogram bars from bar spacing. Not tuned.
- **Volume in PNG export** is included when on; not separately verified.
- **Line count:** `TradeChart.tsx` and `TradeDetail.tsx` stay grandfathered over 450. Growth is +10 and +7 lines, within the caps.
