## What it does

- `/strategy-lab` (Mark tab only): date, ET minute (±1m), price (tick 0.25, ±tick), side, account. "Build context card" loads up to 12 recent 1m sessions via `lib/strategy-lab/bars.ts`, builds the card, and renders the five groups.
- **Groups** (spec §12.2 order): Candle / MTF (open), Levels & targets, Stops & R, Overnight · Yesterday · Week, Volume (open). Each row has an ⓘ with the skill's definition and version, and a named reason when a value is missing.
- **At the mark vs after the mark:** the decision bar is the one that closed at the marked minute (SLQ-15). The only after-mark fact is the first 1m reclaim within 60 bars in the same session, shown as a muted "After the mark" row (e.g. the 08:13 bar closes at 08:14, so the row reads 08:14 ET).
- **R:** the R size comes from `labRSize` (account, else `prefs.riskBudgetUsd`, else $250). The basis line is shown once per screen: "R = $300 (SIM Eval 50K)" or "R = $250 (default risk budget)".
- **Shield (UX-64):** every dollar row is dropped, per-contract $ notes are omitted, and the ⓘ text is title and version only (levelMap's definition says "$ per contract"). No "$" appears anywhere in the card. R and points stay.
- **Volume 0** is missing (NaN) in the series. The data line counts those bars, and no volume row reads 0.
- **States:** `deriveCardState` returns loading, error, offline, partial, or ready. All five are rendered and were checked headless.
- No MFE or MAE rows: the card uses no measure-kind skill, and a test asserts that.

## Choices the spec does not settle (for the planner)

1. **File split** as above (`context-card-rows.ts`, `et-clock.ts`).
2. **Shield and the ⓘ text:** title and version only under Shield. The alternative is to rewrite skill definitions, which touches SL-3/4/5 files, so I did not.
3. **After-mark window:** 60 1m bars, same session only. Shows the bar close time.
4. **"Contracts at 0.25R":** the quantity comes from `structureStop` at riskR 0.25. The note shows the actual risk at that quantity (for example 0.24R at 3 ct), not the target.
5. **R:R to nearest above** is computed in the card model (level distance ÷ stop distance), not by a skill. It is labelled "to <level>".
6. **Levels group** shows the nearest level above and below (levelMap), the RTH VWAP (vwapRelation), and the opening range. There is no per-level list.
7. **Error versus cache:** the card goes to error only when no session loads at all. Completed sessions are cached on the device (§10.2), so a past mark can still build a card while Yahoo is down.
8. **Find similar / Save as recipe** are disabled placeholders (SL-8).
9. **Not built** (not in SL-7's acceptance): the playbook selector, `?trade=<id>` prefill, and the Recipes and Runs tabs (§6.3, §12.1).
