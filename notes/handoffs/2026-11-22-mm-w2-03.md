## What was built

1. **Pure Types (`lib/partials.ts`):**
   - `PartialExit`: executed exit legs with `time`, `price`, `qty`, `reason`, `targetKey`, `orderId`, `seq`.
   - `PartialTarget`: planned targets with allocated quantity, reward distance, points, USD P&L, and planned R.
   - `PartialLotBand`: visual and financial band for each lot or closed FIFO slice with entry/exit coordinates (`entryTime`, `exitTime`, `t0`, `t1`, `entryPrice`, `exitPrice`, `p0`, `p1`), integer-tick financials (`ticks`, `contractTicks`, `points`, `pnlCents`, `pnlUsd`), per-lot risk & R (`r`, `rContribution`), and layout metadata (`shareOfPosition`, `opacityMultiplier`, `isOpen`, `exitReason`, `targetKey`).
   - `PartialSummary`: aggregated multi-lot execution summary with price averages, initial risk metrics, contract ticks, avg ticks, points, integer-cent P&L, `rMultiple` (weighted total R), `realizedR`, `closedR`, `isScratch`, bands, exits, and targets.

2. **Integer-Tick Financial Math (Guardrail 4):**
   - Strict integer-tick calculations across multiple exit legs using contract specs (`MES` = $5/pt, `MNQ` = $2/pt, `ES` = $50/pt, `NQ` = $20/pt).
   - Zero floating-point money math: all dollar amounts derived from exact integer ticks and cents (`pnlCents = contractTicks * tickValueCents`).
   - Off-tick prices and non-finite numbers fail closed, returning `null` (never plausible floating math).

3. **Per-Lot R Contribution & Total Weighted R (Fixture 7 Verified):**
   - Per-lot R: `lotR = lotTicks / initialRiskTicksPerContract`.
   - Per-lot contribution: `rContribution = (qty / totalEntryQty) * lotR`.
   - Verified against Fixture 7 (`ex-partials.json`): 2 lots MES entered at 7600.00 with initial stop at 7595.00 (5 pts = 20 ticks = $25/lot risk, $50 total risk).
     * T1 hit at 7605.00: 1 lot, +5.0 pts (+20 ticks = +$25.00, +1.0R, contribution +0.50R).
     * T2 hit at 7612.50: 1 lot, +12.5 pts (+50 ticks = +$62.50, +2.5R, contribution +1.25R).
     * Total contract ticks: 70, P&L: +$87.50 (+8,750 cents).
     * Total weighted R: `+1.75R` (`0.50R + 1.25R = 1.75R`, and `$87.50 / $50.00 = 1.75R`).

4. **Lot-Band Geometry for PositionBoxPrimitive / buildMarks (Lane 8):**
   - Generates `PartialLotBand[]` with canonical coordinate pairs (`t0`, `t1`, `p0`, `p1`) and timing (`entryTime`, `exitTime`).
   - Sets `shareOfPosition` and `opacityMultiplier` scaling with the position share per `docs/15 §2.4`.
   - FIFO matching connects exit fills against entry fills (supporting scale-ins and multi-leg exits).

5. **Stop Absence & Open Lot Guards:**
   - When initial stop is absent, `R === null` (never 0).
   - When stop is placed on the wrong side (or at entry with 0 distance), `R === null` (never positive distance).
   - When open lots remain, open bands are emitted with `isOpen: true`, null exit coordinates, and null P&L/R; `summary.isClosed` is `false`, `summary.rMultiple` is `null`, while `summary.realizedR` and `summary.closedR` report realized progress.

6. **Scratch Evaluation:**
   - Implemented `isScratchPartials` honoring owner decision `docs/15 §1.3`: within `0.1R` or `2 ticks` of entry, whichever is larger (or explicit breakeven exit reason).
