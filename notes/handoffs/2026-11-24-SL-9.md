## Choices the spec left open (reversible)

1. **Win rate = wins ÷ n**, where n counts every match and scratches are not wins. The Wilson interval uses the same n.
2. **Scratch** = |P&L| ≤ 2 ticks × qty, using MES defaults (tick 0.25, $5/pt → $2.50 per contract). `tickSize` and `pointValueUsd` are options.
3. **Gating** (SLQ-9): n < 5 shows counts only (rates, Wilson, avg R and shrunk R are `null`); 5–9 shows rates with `thin: true`; ≥ 10 is full. Separators use n ≥ 10 per bucket, or n ≥ 5 when the run has fewer than 40 matches, and are then flagged `thin`.
4. **Shrinkage** `(n × avgR + 10 × overallAvgR) ÷ (n + 10)`, with the overall average over all matches.
5. **Clarity**: best and worst buckets (by shrunk avg R) are `clear` when their Wilson intervals do not overlap, otherwise `suggestive`.
6. **Stability**: sessions are split chronologically at the middle session date. Each half's best bucket is the one with the highest shrunk avg R. `stable` when both halves agree. A half with fewer than two populated buckets is `unstable`.
7. **Ranking**: clear before suggestive, then stable before unstable, then larger `gapR`, then the slice key. Clarity is the primary key because §8.4 names it first. This is the most likely thing to change.
8. **Buckets**: a skill's declared `buckets` give `< e0`, `e0–e1`, …, `≥ ek` (so 1.3 starts the high bucket, as the spec's RVOL edges require). Without declared buckets, numeric slices use terciles of the run (`low`, `mid`, `high`). Categories and booleans use their declared order, then unseen values. Null values form an `__na` bucket labelled "n/a", always last and only present when it has members, so nothing is dropped.
9. **Time of day** assumes `Match.time` is the trigger bar's **open** time. The decision time is `time + 60 s` (bar close, §4.4) and it is bucketed to ET 30-minute blocks. SL-8 must set `Match.time` that way, or this must change.
10. **Feature keys**: `featureKey(ref, major = 1)` returns `skill@major:out(k=v,…)` with params sorted. SL-8 (runner) must build `Match.features` keys with this same function. Pass `featureKeyOf` to override.
11. **Output metadata** comes from the optional `outputSpec(ref)` callback, so SL-8 can pass the registry lookup. Without it the kind is inferred from the values.
