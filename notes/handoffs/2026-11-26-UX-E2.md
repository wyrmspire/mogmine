## What changed

- **Price-axis tags (D-V3).** `priceAxisViews()` tags the selected trade only. Archived marks are excluded. Order is entry, stop, target, exit. Colours come from the theme chips: entry is neutral, stop is risk, target is reward, exit is reward or risk by outcome. An exit that equals entry, stop or target adds no tag. Off-pane prices return `-1000` and `visible()` clips to `[0, paneHeight]`.
- **R ruler (§4.2, §5).** Ruler segments paint `DASH.ruler` `[2, 4]`, butt caps, 0.35 alpha in `labelMuted`. Ruler labels paint as quiet `10px` mono text in `labelMuted` at alpha 0.8, at `atX + 4`, vertically centred. They are no longer queued as pills.
- **Chip leaders (§4.3).** A chip moved more than 2 px by `separateLabels` gets a 1 px leader at 0.6 alpha in `chip.bg`, from `(x - 5, anchor y)` to `(x, chip y)`. Both ends are snapped with `crisp`.
- **Export mode (§4.3).** `setExportMode(true)` makes chips 18 px tall with 11.5 px text. The label stack uses 20 px. `setExportMode(false)` restores 16 px and 10.5 px. Each call requests a repaint. UX-E4 is the caller.

## Judgement calls (please check)

1. **`drawFlag` and `strokeLine` moved to `lib/chart-paint.ts`.** Without the move, `PositionBoxPrimitive.ts` would be over 450 lines. The move is code-identical.
2. **Entry tag** takes the first `tone === "entry"` segment's `p0`, falling back to the first actual zone's `p0`. Entry segments in the plan layer (pending working orders) count. Exit is the actual zone's far price, furthest from entry.
3. **Target tag** is the planned profit zone whose far price is furthest from its inner price.
4. **Shield min width 44 px** exists as a constant and as the `chipWidth` `minWidth` parameter. It is not wired to any chip yet. Shield chips are not in this package.
5. `setExportMode` is called with no argument, which means `false`.
