# [R3-11] HR-5 stop slippage, HR-10 derived read-back, and price_out_of_range from loaded bars

**Migration:** none. No Supabase objects, no migrations, no data touched.

**Scope:** `MOGMINE-BLUEPRINT.md` §3.5 item 5 (HR-5 trigger vs slipped fill), `docs/design-2026-11-24/07-agent-api.md` §2–§4. Depends on R3-07.

## What changed

- `lib/agent/house-rules.ts`: `HOUSE_RULES_VERSION` is `2026-11-26.1`.
  - HR-5 now says `stop`/`trail` mean the working stop triggered; `exit_price` is the actual fill and may slip adversely. True `exit_price` and `stop_loss_price` are kept, never moved. A clean favorable/untriggered exit is `manual` or `target`, not `stop`.
  - HR-10 lists the agent-API derived names (`pnl_cents`, `result_r`, `risk_cents`, `risk_r`, `r_size_cents`, `hold_seconds`, `session_date`), says they are never written, and points to `?include=derived`. The stale "until then, do not expect" phrase is gone.
  - Rules stay HR-1..HR-12 in order. The word "service", "showmob", "mmk_" and "trade-2026" do not appear.
- `lib/agent/write-checks.ts`: the exit check is side-aware. Code stays `exit_reason_mismatch`; `EXIT_TICK_TOLERANCE = 2` stays.
  - Adverse slip beyond 2 ticks: "slipped N ticks past working stop … Keep both true prices … or add stop_moves (HR-4, HR-5)".
  - Favorable/untriggered beyond 2 ticks: "sits N ticks on the favorable (untriggered) side … use manual or target … or record the stop_moves trail".
- `lib/agent/trade-check.ts`: `runTradeCheck` computes `dayPriceRange` from the loaded bars (null when there are none) and passes it to `checkTradeWrite`, so `price_out_of_range` now fires from the check. No new loads and no extra calls.
- `lib/agent/openapi.ts`: the hardcoded HR-5 wording in the write-rule reminder now matches the new rule. The `exit_reason_mismatch (HR-5)` mention is unchanged.
- `lib/__tests__/agent-slippage-and-check.test.ts` (new): HR-5/HR-10 text, the house-rules guard, LONG and SHORT exit cases (2/6 ticks adverse, 5/6 ticks favorable), manual exit silent, and `price_out_of_range` for entry 4866.25 against bars at 7110–7145. A within-range entry is silent.

Not touched: `lib/agent/dispatch.ts`, `lib/stop-facts.ts`, `supabase/migrations/**`, PR #71, PR #271, `feat/databento-mes-1m`.
