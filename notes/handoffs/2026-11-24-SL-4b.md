## Choices I made (reversible)

1. **Branch fast-forwarded to `origin/main` (25db77a) before starting.** `sl/SL-4b` was an ancestor 12 commits behind, and SL-4a's LevelBook lives on main.
2. **Default gap policy is `globex-tolerant` (IN-2), RTH strict.** Callers can pass `complete`.
3. **Tolerance constants are mirrored** (3% missing, 15-minute gap) because `session-indicators.ts` does not export `GLOBEX_MAX_*`. The boundary tests pin both sides (6 of 200 passes, 7 fails; 15-minute gap passes, 16 fails) against the reference, so drift shows up. Follow-up: export them from `session-indicators.ts` (shared file, not in this package).
4. **Volume:** a present bar with missing volume (0 on the source) counts for coverage but adds nothing to the sum. The reference's `volumesUsable` clause (non-finite or negative volume withholds VWAP) is not replicated, because the lab series has already mapped those to NaN. Fixtures never hit that case.
5. **S8 width bucket** uses terciles of the prior 20 sessions' complete ranges, and is null with fewer than 6. The spec's "else run terciles" needs run-level state the skill does not have, so the runner should compute it if wanted.
6. **S7 position** is not clamped, so a mark outside the overnight range gives <0 or >100. Position is null when the range is zero. The yesterday "flat" band is |close − open| < 0.1% of open.
7. **S9 event** compares the previous bar's close with its VWAP and this close with this VWAP, strictly. The "at" band is ±2 ticks. Slope is (VWAP now − VWAP five minutes earlier) ÷ 5, points per minute, within the same session.
