## Done

- **S1 `sessionClock`** (label, v1): `phase`, `todBucket`, `minutesFromRthOpen` on the decision clock (bar close, America/New_York, from `BarSeries.etMinute`). The 17:00–18:00 halt returns `out-of-session`.
- **S2 `mtfCandle`** (feature, v1): the ET-aligned 5m/15m/60m candle containing bar `i` (forming or complete), its range/body/wicks/`bodyPct`, midrank percentiles against prior completed candles, `direction`, `forming`, and the same set for the last completed candle (`prev*`). - 25 tests in one file, with the known-at harness (`assertNoLookahead`) on all four skills. Each expected value is hand-computed from the synthetic bars in its test.

## Choices the spec did not settle (for the planner to record in DECISIONS.md)

1. **Phase clock is the decision time (bar close).** A bar opening 09:29 decides at 09:30 → `rthOpen`. A bar opening 16:59 decides at 17:00 → `post`. So `post` runs 16:00–17:00 on the close clock, where §9 S1 wrote "16:00–16:59". The phase table is in `clock.ts`, and the skill's `warnings` says so.
2. **`todBucket`** is the 30-minute bucket of the decision time, not the bar open.
3. **`minutesFromRthOpen`** is measured to the nearest 09:30 open in (−720, 720]. §9 only says "negative before".
4. **`prev*` in S2** is the last *completed* candle, read literally. When the current candle has ended, `prev*` equals the current values. While it forms, `prev*` is the candle before it.
5. **Prior-candle buckets are not session-checked.** Bucket boundaries sit on the hour (5/15/60 divide 60, and 18:00 and 17:00 are on the hour), so no bucket straddles a session.
6. **S3 structure.** A candle with more than 20% missing minutes cannot be part of a swing's neighbourhood. "Two lower lows" before the latest low means two consecutive lower-low comparisons.
7. **S4 extreme.** It is the most extreme 1m low (long; high for short) among the `lookbackBars` bars *before* `i`, with ties going to the latest bar. That keeps `j < i` by construction. A lower low after `j`, including at `i`, means no reclaim.
8. **S4 `side` parameter added.** §9 says "short mirrors" but lists no side parameter, so `side` (`long` default, or `short`) is a skill param. For short, the output key `higherLow` means a lower 1m high (the mirror), with the key kept as the spec names it.
9. **Skills are not registered in `registry.ts` `SKILLS`.** That file is foundation, and SL-3's row lists only the two new files. The skills are exported as `sessionClockSkill` and `MTF_SKILLS`, and the test shows they compose with `createSkillRegistry`. Adding them to `SKILLS` belongs to SL-8, or to the integrator.
10. **Known gaps:** `mtfCandle` recomputes up to 2 × `pctileLookback` buckets per call, with no cache. That is fine for the MVP. Caching belongs with the bar cache (SL-2 / SL-15) if the scan is slow.
