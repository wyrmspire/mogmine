## What changed

- `lib/entry-form.ts`: `DraftPartialTarget`, `DraftPartialExit`, optional `partialTargets` / `partialExits` on `EntryDraft` (kept by `upgradeDraft`; a confirmed save already clears the draft). New pure helpers `resolveDraftPartials` (validation, weighted exit price on the tick grid, latest leg time, exact metrics via `partialsTradeMetrics` when legs close every contract), `remainingOpenQty`, and `scaleOutTradePatch`.
- `components/EntryPartialsSection.tsx` (new): collapsible "Split targets / partial exits (optional)", `+ Add target (T n)`, `+ Add partial exit`, tick steppers, 44px tap targets, `tabular-nums`, and `password` masking on contract inputs under Shield.
