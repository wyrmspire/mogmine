# FB-1 — ±5-minute fill-to-bar matching

Swarm lane L2 (`swarm-2`), Wave 1. Branch `swarm/FB-1`. Claim `refs/heads/claim/FB-1`.

**No migration in this PR.** Pure `lib/` change, no schema.

## What changed

- **`lib/fill-match.ts` (new, 113 lines, pure).** `matchFill` and `matchTradeFills` per `05-stops-mae-fills.md` §4.2. A fill is matched to the bar its price traded in, within ±5 minutes (one bar each side on 5m/15m/1h). Ties go to the earlier bar for an entry and the later bar for an exit. The entry is never matched after the exit.
- **`lib/trade-bar-validation.ts` (382 → 424 lines).** `observeTrade` matches each checkable fill in time order with a monotonic `notBefore`. A `shifted` fill emits `fill_time_shifted` (no `fill_outside_bar`) and takes its matched bar as its effective time for position windows (D-I). Stored `entry_time`, `exit_time` and `position_timeline[].t` are never written (the timeline is a copy). `ObservedTrade.fillMatches` carries the matches to the marks.
- **`lib/trade-marks.ts` (509 → 524 lines, grandfathered).** Fill flags, fill lines, stop hatches and lot spans anchor on the matched bar with `approximate: true` when shifted (bucketed by `anchor` for 5m/15m). `fill_time_shifted` never becomes an on-chart flag. The explanation lives in Data notes.
- **`lib/data-notes.ts` (93 → 118 lines).** `fill_time_shifted` becomes a quiet note: `Entry drawn on the 14:13 bar; you logged 14:14.` It carries an `action` of kind `use_matched_fill`, with `matchedBarTime` and label `Use 14:13`, for FB-2. Nothing is rendered or applied here.
- **`lib/__tests__/fill-match.test.ts` (new, 12 tests).** Covers §4.4 and §5 row C: stated; trade C (−1 shift, observer, marks and note in `America/Chicago`); tie-break at −2/+2; 4-tick stated; outside; order constraint; no-bars; 5m bucketing; no mutation.

## Judgement calls (for Chris and the integrator)

1. **A shift beats the 4-tick tolerance on the stated bar.** This is the order in `05` §4.2 (step 2 before step 3). Consequence: a fill that is within 4 ticks of its logged bar but exactly inside a neighbour bar now moves to that neighbour. This is intended, and worth a look.
2. **A missing logged bar is never judged `outside`.** `05` §4.2 step 6 reads literally as "outside fires". Applied to a missing bar, `alignToBar` anchors on a bar up to 3 minutes away, which produced a false `fill_outside_bar` in the existing `ex-partials` test. The guard keeps the old behaviour: no finding when the logged bar is absent. A shift from a missing bar is still allowed.
3. **Note time text is `HH:MM` (`formatListTime`), not `formatDetailTime`.** `formatDetailTime` adds seconds and a zone label (`14:13:00 CT`), which does not match the `Entry drawn on the 14:13 bar` text in the spec. The existing `fill_outside_bar` sentence still uses `formatDetailTime`, unchanged.
4. **The shifted note has no `≈` prefix**, to match the spec's example text.
5. **Stated fills are recorded in `fillMatches` with `shifted: false`.** Their timestamps are not moved, so position windows behave as before.
